EVENTS WE ORGANISED

Conferences, unconferences and community events across Belgium and the Netherlands, with international speakers and room for peer exchange.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Organisations that learn can adapt. That learning grows from practitioners sharing their experience with each other.

LEARNING FROM PRACTICE

Real experience teaches more than theory. We create space where practitioners share what works, what doesn't, and why.

ADAPTIVE ORGANISATIONS

Organisations that sense, experiment and adjust. We explore ways of working that help teams and organisations adapt to change.

PEER EXCHANGE

Learning is a two-way street. Participants shape the conversation and learn from each other, with formats like Open Space Technology and Liberating Structures.

OPEN & TRANSPARENT

We work as volunteers, in the open. We share what we learn, including what did not work, and we welcome new ideas and feedback.

ABOUT RE-EVENTED

Re-Evented was formed in 2016 by agile practitioners who wanted to create different kinds of learning moments, where practitioners share what works, what doesn't, and why. As a non-profit, every decision serves the people we learn with.

We experiment with formats, reflect on what works, and adapt. We organised conferences, unconferences and workshops across the Benelux, and we keep sharing what those years taught us about learning and adaptive organisations.

10 years. Dozens of events. Hundreds of practitioners.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is Re-Evented? +

Re-Evented is a Belgian-Dutch non-profit organisation for learning and adaptive organisations: agile methods, lean thinking, reinventing organisations, and emerging technologies. Founded in 2016, it brings practitioners together to share what works. Over the years it organised events such as XP Days Benelux, the Regional Scrum Gathering, the Liberating Structures Global Gathering, and AI Summit Brussels.

Who runs Re-Evented? +

Re-Evented is run by a volunteer team of agile practitioners from Belgium and the Netherlands. It operates as two legal entities: a Belgian non-profit association (VZW) and a Dutch foundation (Stichting), both founded in 2016.
