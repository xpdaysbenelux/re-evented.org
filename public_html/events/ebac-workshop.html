Your Workshop Leaders

Bob Galen

🖥️ Joining remotely

President & Principal Agile Coach, RGalen Consulting

Bob Galen is a Certified Enterprise Coach – Emeritus (CEC-Em) with over 20 years of hands-on agile coaching experience. He is widely recognised as a "Coach of Coaches" and one of the leading voices of pragmatic agility. Bob has delivered around 400 conference presentations, 15 keynotes, and authored several books including Extraordinarily Badass Agile Coaching, Scrum Product Ownership, and Agile Reflections.

As both an external consultant and internal change agent, Bob has held senior leadership roles driving organisational transformation across a wide range of industries. He is also an ORSC-trained Organisation Relationship Systems Coach and a Leadership Circle Certified Practitioner.

rgalen.com

Peter Fischbach

📍 Present live in Brussels

Executive Scrum Coach & Scrum Fellow

Peter Fischbach is an executive Scrum coach based in Berlin, Germany. He collaborates closely with Scrum co-creator Jeff Sutherland to teach teams and executives across Europe. In 2019, Sutherland nominated Peter as one of only 12 Scrum Fellows worldwide, tasked with spreading Scrum knowledge globally.

Since 2015, Peter has worked with Sutherland on Scrum@Scale and is one of the first Registered Scrum@Scale Trainers™ worldwide. A former ensemble coach and stage actor, Peter brings a unique focus on embracing Scrum values first. His coaching spans software, banking, mobility, logistics, and scientific research. He is the author of Scrum in AI, with a foreword by Jeff Sutherland.

scrum-events.de
