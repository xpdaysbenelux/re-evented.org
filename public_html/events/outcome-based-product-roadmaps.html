Your Roadmap Should Drive Outcomes,
Not Just List Features

Most product roadmaps are little more than feature wishlists with dates attached. They create false certainty, drive output-focused delivery, and disconnect product work from the value it should create.

In this workshop, Roman Pichler will guide you through a practical, outcome-based approach to product roadmapping — one that connects vision to value and empowers teams to make better product decisions.

What You'll Explore

A hands-on workshop covering the essential skills for creating and managing outcome-based product roadmaps.

01  From Outputs to Outcomes

Understand why feature-based roadmaps fail and how outcome-based roadmaps create alignment between product strategy, user needs, and business goals.

02  Connecting Vision to Value

Learn how to bridge the gap between your product vision and the measurable value you deliver — creating a coherent chain from vision through outcomes to impact.

03  Crafting Effective Outcomes

Practice defining outcomes that are specific, measurable, and genuinely useful for guiding product decisions — avoiding vanity metrics and vague aspirations.

04  Managing Roadmaps as Living Tools

Explore how to keep your roadmap alive — reviewing progress, adapting to new insights, and communicating changes to stakeholders without losing trust.

Who Should Attend

  • → Product managers and product owners looking to move beyond feature-driven roadmaps
  • → Product leaders responsible for strategy and portfolio alignment
  • → Scrum masters and agile coaches supporting product teams
  • → Anyone involved in product decisions who wants to connect daily work to meaningful outcomes

Your Workshop Leader

Roman Pichler

Product management expert & author

Roman Pichler is one of Europe's leading product management experts. He specialises in product strategy and roadmapping, and has helped countless product teams and organisations create successful products.

Roman is the author of several widely-read books including Strategize, How to Lead in Product Management, and Agile Product Management with Scrum. He created tools such as the Product Vision Board and the GO Product Roadmap, used by product teams around the world.
